How it works

01

Register with a code

Create a patient record without entering a name.

02

Screen in a few minutes

Ask two quick questions, then follow up with the PHQ-9 when indicated.

03

Plan the next step

An alert helps the specialist review the case and book a visit when needed.

Designed around each role

Who it's for

NCD nurse

Registers with a code, screens, and sees their own screening list.

Psychiatric nurse

Receives a fair share of alerts, follows up, and escalates complex cases.

Clinical psychologist

Reviews specialist referrals from health centers in their district.

Privacy by role

Only what is needed.

Patient records use codes instead of names. Phone numbers are encrypted in the database. Each role has different access, and access to alerts and appointments is logged.
